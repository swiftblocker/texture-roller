## Coverage gaps

- No measured depth-per-clay-state data anywhere; every depth figure is a designer default or a vendor claim, none a clay test.
- Reddit not read at all (no source from r/ceramics, r/3Dprinting, r/Pottery).
- Etsy, Printables and MakerWorld pages blocked (403/CAPTCHA); S051, S061-S066 are search snippets only. Real 3D-printed-roller makes and maker comments are missing.
- No ceramic-specific slab thickness, sticking/tearing mechanics, or glaze pooling data in mm; no drying shrinkage % (only fired shrinkage).
- No source tests PLA vs PETG vs resin against wet clay, or sealing of a printed roller for clay contact (S070, S071 are generic food contact).
- No CNC roller data beyond one 4-axis makerspace page (S110): no bit diameters, V-bit angles, RPM, or minimum feature width; no wood/MDF/acrylic roller carving sources.
- No seam-tiling or repeat tests; seam facts are generator formulas and one hobby claim.
- No data on bearing/axle fit or wall thickness vs rolling load for a 254 mm (10 in) roller; longest printed roller seen is 180 mm (S060, S061); longest hand roller is a 16 in dowel (S049).
- RH-1 handle dimensions are not given by any source.
