# S020: Texture rollers/stamps/3D printed rollers... What works best? (The Crafting Brothers)

- Date read: 2026-11-24
- Type: youtube
- Trust (1-5): 3
## Key claims
- Test surfaces compared: XPS foam (hardest to roll, least detail), dollar-store foam (about twice the detail of XPS), air-dry/modeling clay and Model Magic/putty (about twice the detail of dollar foam, best overall).
- Clay needs a release agent such as baby powder, otherwise a 3D printed roller sticks to clay.
- 3D printed rolling stamps cannot be used on clay; they stick even with baby powder (tested on Model Magic).
- Model Magic and putty warp; glue or attach the clay slab to a surface as soon as possible. Standard modeling clay breaks easily once warped.
- Rolling into clay needs little pressure, so a glove is unnecessary.
- No dimensions, depth or layer-height figures given.
- Tip: scoring with an X-acto and tracing with a ballpoint pen gives a brick pattern without a roller; heated tin foil on a soldering iron deepens the texture on XPS foam.
- Finish: mod podge base, dry-brush, white highlight. Paint shows the texture through.
