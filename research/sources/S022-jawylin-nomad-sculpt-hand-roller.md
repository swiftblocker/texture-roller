## Key claims
- Modeled in Nomad Sculpt on iPad (units mm). Roller core cylinder: height 127 mm (about 5 in), radius 50 mm (100 mm diameter), edges sharpened with division. Take reference measurements from a roller you own.
- Snap set to 90 degrees to keep the roller level when rotating.
- Two handles: cloned from a cylinder, scaled smaller, mirrored across X. Warning: handles too thick can jam against the clay and stop the roller turning freely.
- Designs added with stamp tool after subdividing the roller (multi-resolution) to avoid a blocky look.
- Raised vs recessed: sub (subtract) makes an indentation in the roller, which pushes raised relief into clay. Text must be mirrored, or it prints backwards on clay.
- Voxel remesh at 500 resolution to merge layers; decimate to keep under about 100,000 vertices so the slicer does not slow.
- Print: Bambu A1 with AMS, supports needed for the middle roller. Handles printed separately with snap connectors (cut tool, snap connector, centred on geometry). Roughly 1.5 h for the roller.
- Admission: designs should be deeper; the relief barely showed on the clay in the final test. Test a small version first.
- Workflow tip: save a plain base roller file and reuse it for new stamps.
