# S049: Ceramic Arts Network, "In the Studio: Texture Rollers"
- URL: https://ceramicartsnetwork.org/pottery-making-illustrated/pottery-making-illustrated-article/in-the-studio--texture-rollers
- Date read: 2026-11-24
- Type: maker
- Trust (1-5): 3

## Key claims
- Maker's roller: wooden dowel 1 1/4 in x 48 in (round), cut to 16 in (40.6 cm) rollers; length flexible. Diameter about 32 mm.
- Core: solid wooden dowel.
- Pattern: 2 mm adhesive-backed craft foam cut by hand and pressed onto the dowel. Pen marks give a softer, subtler impression than cut foam edges.
- Material note: author tried PVC pipe; clay stuck to PVC more than wood.
- Handle: none (hand rolled).
- Relief depth: foam pattern 2 mm (effective relief).
- Clay state: rolls across a "freshly rolled slab."
- Rolling: even pressure; rotate roller or vary start/end points to reduce repetition.
- Dusting, cleaning, food safety: not mentioned.
- Price: none.

## Quotes (each under 15 words, max 3)
- "Apply even pressure for more predictable results."
- "Rotate the roller or change where each pass begins and ends."

## Usage notes
- Same roller works on small or large slabs at the same design scale.
