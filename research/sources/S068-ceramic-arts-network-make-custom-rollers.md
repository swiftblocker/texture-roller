## Key claims
- Method is ceramic, not printed: rollers are ceramic cylinders with lines, dots, textures or stamps pressed in, then bisque fired.
- No roller diameter, length, relief depth or handle/axle method given.
- Seam advice (for slabs, not rollers): over-bend slabs over a large-diameter pipe, because slabs "like to open up as they dry"; score, slip and compress joins with a rib or brayer, without damaging texture.
- No sealing, food-safety or 3D-print content.
- Useful as a non-printed baseline for roller design and for what a clay-facing surface must do.
