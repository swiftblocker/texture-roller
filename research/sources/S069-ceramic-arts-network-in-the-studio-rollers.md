## Key claims
- Core: 1-1/4 inch x 48 inch wooden dowel, cut into three 16 inch (about 40.6 cm) rollers for large platters; length varies with slab size.
- Relief: 2 mm adhesive-backed craft foam glued onto the dowel; foam shapes are the impression. Relief depth stated only as the 2 mm foam thickness.
- Handle/axle: none; bare dowel acts as the core and axle.
- Material: wood beat PVC. Author tried PVC pipe first; clay stuck more to PVC than to wood.
- Ballpoint-pen marks in foam give a softer, subtler impression than cut edges.
- No sealing, food-safety, print settings, or seam/tearing notes.
- Relevant to the project: confirms clay sticking differs by roller surface material; wood core is the author's choice.
