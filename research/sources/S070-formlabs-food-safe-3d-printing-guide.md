## Key claims
- Vendor blog (Formlabs sells resins), so treat product-leaning statements with care.
- FDM layer lines leave grooves between layers; groove depth grows with layer height. Lowest practical layer height recommended for food contact.
- Bacteria in grooves is the main FDM concern; smooth surface needed for long-term food safety.
- FDM parts can be chemically smoothed (acetone, d-limonene, ethyl acetate), but a food-safe coating is still recommended afterward.
- Common food-safe sealants listed: food-grade epoxy or polyurethane, or FDA-approved PTFE, applied by dip coating.
- Coatings do not guarantee safety: can degrade, may not be dishwasher safe.
- PLA/PETG: food-safe filaments exist but approval varies by brand and region; PLA softens and distorts around 60-70 C; not dishwasher safe; not for hot liquids.
- Use stainless steel nozzle, not brass (lead concern).
- Bleach and microwaving are not effective for sanitising printed parts.
- Clay-specific use is not covered.
