## Key claims
- Pure PLA generally considered food-safe; cited 1995 study found it GRAS for food contact.
- Colored PLA may contain non-food-safe dyes or additives; undyed filament preferred; check the manufacturer's safety data sheet.
- Brass nozzle may contain lead; stainless steel nozzle considered food-safe.
- Epoxy sealing: coating must be FDA-certified food-safe and fully cured. Liquid epoxy is toxic (gloves and mask).
- One cited test: epoxy-coated prints showed no bacterial growth after 14 days of use and washing; uncoated PLA showed significant growth.
- Silicone also seals pores; less hazardous than epoxy.
- FDM pores between layers trap moisture and particles; article recommends treating PLA utensils as single-use.
- Coatings wear down; some not dishwasher-safe or suited to hot food.
- PLA cannot be boiled or dishwashed.
- Clay-specific use is not covered.
