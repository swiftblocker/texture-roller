# S080: How to Make Custom Clay Texture Rollers (Sarah Pike, Ceramic Arts Network)

## Key claims
- Author: Sarah Pike. Published 2025-02-21 (first published 2017).
- Rollers are ceramic cylinders with texture pushed in, then bisque fired.
- Stamps carved from leather-hard clay. Slabs rolled or stamped while soft and used immediately.
- Clay body not named. No mm, inch, slab thickness, relief depth, shrinkage % or firing temperature given. Pipe described only as "large-diameter".
- No release agent mentioned. Slabs transferred onto cement board (HardieBacker) before texturing.
- Slabs over-bent to build flex and avoid seam tension; joins scored, slipped and compressed with a rib or brayer, taking care not to damage texture.
- Undercuts and sharp edges not addressed. Carved stamps reverse the imprint, so the potter must decide which areas are raised (catch glaze) and which recessed (glaze pools).
## Quotes (each under 15 words, max 3)
- "The tricky part is deciding which areas will be positive and negative."
