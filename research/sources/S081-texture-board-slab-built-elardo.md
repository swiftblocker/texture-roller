# S081: How to Make a Texture Board for Slab-Built Pottery (Gale Batsimm, Ceramic Arts Network)

## Key claims
- Author: Gale Batsimm, describing Larry Elardo's method. Published 2020-04-20 (first published 2013).
- Paste layer 3/16 to 1/4 in (about 4.8 to 6.4 mm) thick, set by trowel notch depth. Board dries 24 h before use. Paste workable 15 to 20 min.
- Clay state, slab thickness, impression depth and shrinkage: not given.
- Release: WD-40 or vegetable oil worked into grooves with a toothbrush so clay releases with more detail.
- Press slab down from outside edges toward centre to push clay into grooves. Attach slab firmly so it does not shift.
- Flip board so slab weight releases it; sandwich slab between boards and flip again.
