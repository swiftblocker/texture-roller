## Key claims
- Author: Benjamin Buchenot. Page shows date 2026-10-26.
- Designs CNC-milled into unmounted linoleum. Carving depth not stated.
- Start with a slab thicker than the final target; thickness found by trial and error. No mm given.
- Slab clay state (soft vs leather-hard) not specified.
- Use a slab larger than the mat for even pressure across the whole mat.
- Release: spray mat with cooking oil. May be unnecessary if clay is not tacky and the design is open.
- Dry clay can crack the linoleum and cracks show in the impression. Peel mat from one corner, or halfway from each side.
- Rolling pin with flat sticks on each side controls slab thickness without a slab roller.
