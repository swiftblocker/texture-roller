## Key claims
- Author: Kaitlyn Brennan. Published 2024-07-22 (originally Ceramics Monthly 2018).
- Body: red earthenware. Slab rolled to about 3/8 in (about 9.5 mm) from a 3 lb block.
- Texture made by dragging a faux wood-grain tool across the slab, rolling craft-foam flower and leaf templates in, and drawing vines.
- Slip breaks over the texture and red clay shows through. Depends on slip being at the right thickness.
- Low-temperature electric firings. No texture depth, shrinkage % or firing temperature given.
- Wax resist brushed on foot at leather-hard before dipping, so wet piece can be set down.
