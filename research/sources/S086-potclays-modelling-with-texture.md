## Key claims
- Publisher: Potclays Ltd (supplier). Last updated 2025-12-04.
- Texture work best when clay is "between soft and leather-hard". Too soft distorts; too firm may not take impression.
- Clay types: stoneware durable with clean defined impressions; earthenware softer detail, suited to beginners; porcelain captures fine detail but dries quickly; air-dry clay fragile long term.
- No mm, depth, shrinkage, release agent, tearing or sharp-edge advice.
- Stamp over slip for layered effects.
