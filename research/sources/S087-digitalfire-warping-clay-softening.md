# S087: Digitalfire: Warping (clay softening, porcelain feldspar, glaze fit)

- Date read: 2026-11-24
- Type: reference
- Trust (1-5): 5
## Key claims
- Clay bodies soften as they approach melting point in firing. Porcelain most susceptible (more feldspar). Relevant to texture rounding during firing.
- Porcelain shrinks more on firing because of feldspar content, not whiteness. No firing shrinkage % given on this page.
- Warping: thicker cast walls and a wider, deeper foot ring reduce it; very thin walls contribute.
- Porcelain base recipe roughly 25% each feldspar, silica, kaolin, ball clay. Cone 6 Grolleg ~25% feldspar; NZ or American kaolin ~35%; American kaolin needs 35-40% feldspar for cone 5-6.
- Glaze fit: more silica in clay body improves fit; recommends 20-25% silica. Easier to adjust glaze recipe than clay body.
- No drying shrinkage % given.
