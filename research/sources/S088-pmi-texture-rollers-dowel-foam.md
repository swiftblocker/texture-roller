## Key claims
- Author: Catie Miller. Pottery Making Illustrated, Nov/Dec 2026 issue. No web date shown.
- Build: 1-1/4 in x 48 in (32 mm x 1.2 m) round wood dowel (about $6), cut into three 16 in (40.6 cm) rollers. Roller length 16 in chosen for large platters.
- Pattern: 2 mm adhesive-backed craft foam. Cut-foam edges make a sharper impression than pen marks.
- Optional vinyl cutter (Silhouette or Cricut). PVC pipe tried, not preferred.
- Sticking: clay stuck to PVC more than wood, so wood preferred. Hot glue on a rolling pin hard to control.
- Even pressure gives predictable results on a freshly rolled slab.
- Slab thickness, texture depth, clay moisture state, release agents, tearing, undercuts and sharp edges NOT specified.
