## Key claims
- Defaults (roller.py): size 12 mm; groove 1.2 mm; relief 1.2 mm; grain 0.15 x relief; diameter 30 mm; length 60 mm; bore 8 mm (0 = solid); rim 0 mm; grid res 0.25 mm.
- Circumference rule: one turn prints pi x diameter of imprint (94 mm at 30 mm diameter).
- Relief guidance: 1 to 1.5 mm suits polymer and air-dry clay; more for thick soft-clay slabs.
- FDM (0.4 mm nozzle): keep joint width at 0.8 mm or more.
- Rims 2 to 4 mm recommended to keep depth even across the slab.
- Surface formula: radius = r + relief x (1 - clay), mirrored.
