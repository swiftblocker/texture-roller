# S104: Texture Rollers - for Cookies or Clay (Instructables, rschoenm, 2019)
- URL: https://www.instructables.com/Texture-Rollers-for-Cookies-or-Clay/
- Date read: 2026-11-24
- Type: instructables
- Trust (1-5): 3
## Key claims
- Roller: 40 mm OD, 60 mm tall, sheet-metal wall 2.5 mm, 1 mm gap seam.
- Texture emboss: 2 mm extrusion depth; taper -2 to -5 degrees suggested to help release.
- Seam closure: 0.7 mm join; seam strip extrude 0.5 mm.
- Print: PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 30% infill, 210 C, raft; about 2 h.
- Continuous pattern width must equal pi x diameter (40 mm OD gives 125.66 mm).
- Dough: chill, roll to about 1/8 in, chill again before baking; powdered sugar if sticking.
- Comment example: 50 mm around, 150 mm tall, emboss 2 mm.
## Quotes (each under 15 words, max 3)
- "You can set a taper angle of -2 to -5 degrees."
- "For a 40 mm OD roller the pattern needs to be 125.66 mm wide."
