- Max material: 170 mm long with tailstock, 225 mm without; 136 mm diameter.
- Feed and plunge rate: both 400 mm/min.
- Stepdown: 1.5 mm per pass.
- Stepover: half the bit diameter; straight-groove V-bit 0.12 mm.
- Bit diameters, V-bit angle, RPM and minimum feature width not stated.
- Tailstock recommended for long pieces.
