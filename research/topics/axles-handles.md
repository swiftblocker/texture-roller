# Axles, handles, bores, hollow vs solid

"(weak)" = only trust 1-2 sources back the claim.

## Bores and axles
- Generator bore options: solid, 8 mm rod, 1/2 in or 3/4 in dowel, or custom [S100]; default bore 8 mm (0 = solid) [S101].
- Instructables: bore 20 mm dia x 140 mm long in a 45-50 mm sleeve over a 15 mm x 127 mm rod [S103].
- Metal shaft 1-1.5 mm through a 20 mm roller (copper-wire-style axle) [S065] (weak).
- Bolt-axle style roller with rebate depth and height parameters [S066] (weak).
- Pendant tube bore 5 mm (not a roller) [S027].
- Hand dowel roller: bare 1-1/4 in dowel acts as core and axle, no handle [S049, S069, S088].
- Wood handle was wedged with paper towel after clay loosened [S026].
- Fit tolerance and bearing clearance: no source.

## Handles and frames
- Commercial: MKM RH-1 interchangeable handle fits handle rollers (large, medium, fish) [S044, S046, S047]; sold separately [S047]; recommended for best results with RM roller [S046].
- Xiem rubber rollers swap onto one stainless-steel/hardwood handle; handle sold separately [S040, S041].
- Hand rollers (no handle): MKM LHR and hand rollers are rolled by the hand [S043, S045].
- Handles on printed rollers: two cloned cylinders scaled smaller, mirrored; too thick and they jam against clay [S022]; handles printed separately with snap connectors [S022].
- A separate handle file is included in downloads [S060]; one commenter prefers hands over handles on large rollers [S060] (weak).
- Instructables handle: 26 mm sections (20 mm + 30 mm), 10 degree taper, 5 mm chamfer [S103].
- Handle size for an integrated roller: dowel handle 5 in (127 mm), left longer for grip [S026].
- Frame style: nonstick rubber/plastic on a paint-roller frame [S083].
- Handle ends caps: two-part epoxy putty or polymer end cap [S026].

## Hollow vs solid
- Hollow tube with 3 mm Solidify wall is strong enough for clay rolling [S021].
- Hollow cylindrical roller listed as a tool option [S102].
- Print: 10% infill, 2 walls for a solid-style print of 38/60 mm rollers [S050]; 15% infill [S060]; 15% gyroid [S100]; 30% infill [S104].
- Print time: about 2 h for a 100 x 25 hollow roller [S021]; 1.5 h [S022]; about 8 h per 60x180 roller on an A1 Mini, people ask for lighter versions [S060] (weak).
- Hollow tube cap fill none [S021]; hollow shell too thin until Solidify added [S024] (weak).
- Solid core, wood: 1-1/4 in dowel [S049]. Core not stated for rubber [S040].
- Printed solid cylinder uses 121 g PLA at the listed size [S050].
- Weight vs rolling comfort: no source compares.
